Find scan IDs

Lists the physical buttons you want to identify (one name per line). The hub then loads a series of probe tables onto the remote and asks you to press each button once per round (8 rounds). From which presses get through, it works out each button's scan ID and key press ID. This erases the remote's tables; your project is uploaded again at the end.
